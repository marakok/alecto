How it works

Six steps, one person

Hannah explains your options before every decision. The moments marked “Your call” are yours.

It starts with a tick

Offer accepted. Tell us about the move, verify your identity online and sign your terms. Ten minutes, no paperwork in the post.

Hannah, your conveyancer“Enjoy your first evening. I’m still a message away if you need me.”

Stamp Duty return filed, Land Registry updated, final documents sent.
