Our story

Why we started

Most people move home a handful of times in their life. Every time, the legal part is where it goes quiet: forms, searches, a case handler you never meet.

Alecto turns that around. You get one named conveyancer who reads your file, explains your options in plain English and rings you when something happens. Behind them sits a small team and software that does the chasing, so they have time for you.

We are licensed and regulated by the Council for Licensed Conveyancers, and we work across England and Wales.

What we stand for

Four promises

The same four promises on every move, from a first flat to a family home. (Dummy wording.)

01

One person, start to finish

Your conveyancer is named on day one and stays with you to the keys. No hand-offs, no ticket numbers.

02

Plain English

What’s happening, what it means and what’s next, without the jargon. If something is unusual, we say so.

03

Your call

We lay out the options before every decision. The moments that matter are yours to decide.

04

Finished properly

Stamp Duty filed, Land Registry updated, documents sent. We only close the file when it’s really done.
